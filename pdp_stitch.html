COMPLIMENTARY ARCHIVAL BOXING · INSURED EXPEDITED DISPATCH

Sculpted with a sharp diagonal asymmetric placket that breaks classical Mughal geometry. Features a floating horsehair canvas crafted to mould softly to the contours of the wearer's chest over time, maintaining crisp structural stability without rigid synthetic fusing. Finished with concealed interior silk jet pockets.

Woven on traditional pit-looms in Varanasi utilizing 100% unbleached Mulberry raw silk warp and textured slub weft. The buttons are cast in lost-wax antique brass by fourth-generation metal smiths in Jaipur, hand-carved with our discreet archival lotus glyph.

Each order initiates an individual cut file created exclusively by our master cutter. Clients opting for Made-to-Measure receive a calico toile fitting model prior to final silk cutting. Standard R sizes allow up to two alterations at any Atelier Vayu salon globally.

Delivered encased in a handcrafted deodar cedarwood preservation chest with a museum-grade archival garment bag and hand-turned brass hanger. Covered by Atelier Vayu’s lifetime restorative stitch warranty.
